About Cerium

Built for people who do the work.

Cerium keeps customer inquiries, follow-ups, estimates, jobs, and invoices together, so small crews can spend more time getting work done.

Why we built it

Less paperwork. More time on the job.

Many small crews run on text messages, sticky notes, and memory. Leads get lost, invoices go out late, and it is hard to know who has paid. Cerium gives you one clear page that answers three questions every morning: who to call, who owes you, and what is next.

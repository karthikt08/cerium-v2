Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

You can create an account with email or Google without providing card details. Before using the workspace, you must confirm your phone; some plans may also require Razorpay payment-method authorization.

What happens when the 15-day trial ends?

Your data is never deleted. You can still log in, review existing records, and export your entire workspace as a JSON download. To continue creating new invoices and reconciling bank feeds, authorize your $20/month recurring subscription. No charge is made without authorization.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. You can cancel recurring payments from Workspace Settings at any time.

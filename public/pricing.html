Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

You can create an account with email or Google without providing card details. Before using the workspace, you must confirm your phone; Razorpay payment-method authorization is required before trial access. An eligible UPI AutoPay method may be used in India instead of a credit card.

What happens when the 15-day trial ends?

Your existing records remain available to review and export as JSON, subject to the Privacy Policy. To continue creating or editing business records, maintain an authorised recurring plan: $20/month, or $220/year where annual checkout is enabled for your country. Your billing interval and first scheduled charge are confirmed before authorization.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. You can request cancellation from Workspace Settings at any time; confirm the provider has accepted it.

Win the work

Keep a lead moving from inquiry to a decision without losing the details along the way.

01 / SALES

Lead pipeline

Record people, business enquiries, phone numbers, sources, expected job values and notes. Move each lead through New, Quoted, Booked and Done stages.

02 / MIGRATION

Excel & CSV lead import

Bring existing contacts from .xlsx or .csv spreadsheets. Review suggested column mappings and preview valid rows before confirming. Existing records are not replaced.

03 / PROPOSALS

Estimates and quotes

Build priced estimates with multiple line items, share a secure customer approval link, track accepted or declined quotes, and convert an accepted quote into a scheduled job.

04 / FOLLOW-UP

Callback reminders

Set due dates, tasks and contact methods for each follow-up. See overdue and upcoming work in a simple daily queue.

Tracking and reminders, not automatic SMS campaigns.

Deliver the work

Plan who is going where, when the job starts and what needs to be billed.

05 / SCHEDULING

Jobs and dispatch

Create and edit appointments, monitor job status and view weekly work. Keep service details together instead of spreading them across notes and messages.

06 / TEAMS

Crew assignments

Maintain crew records, assign a worker to a job and spot unassigned bookings on the schedule. No external calendar account required.

07 / BILLING HANDOFF

Completed job to invoice

Turn eligible completed jobs into invoice drafts containing the job’s billable line items. You still review the prices and customer information before issuing.

Drafting is not automatic sending.

08 / WORKSPACE

A focused daily overview

See the leads that need attention, callbacks that are due, outstanding amounts, overdue invoices and review tasks from one dashboard.

Get paid and follow through

Make the paperwork clear and keep your next customer conversation organised.

09 / INVOICING

Country-aware invoices

Create itemised invoices with issue and due dates, payment terms and editable tax fields. INR/GST workflows are available for India; other countries have relevant tax labels and currency formats. Print or save invoices as A4 or US Letter PDFs from your browser.

Review statutory requirements before issuing.

10 / RECEIVABLES

Track what customers owe

Keep invoice records marked Draft, Sent, Overdue or Paid, with outstanding and paid amounts shown in the workspace. Review recorded deposits and any suggested matches where transaction data exists.

Live bank-feed connections are not yet available.

11 / REMINDERS

Approved payment reminder emails

Schedule invoice payment reminders, review which emails should be sent and provide a way for recipients to opt out. Sending depends on an enabled mail worker and configured delivery provider.

Sending is not enabled by default.

12 / RELATIONSHIPS

Review request tracking

Log whether a completed job has a review request, record follow-up status and use your saved Google review link when you ask for feedback.

You choose when to send each request.

Keep control

Choose the tools you need and keep your data portable.

13 / PERSONALISATION

Customisable workspace

Start with an industry-aware tool selection or choose your own modules. Hide tools that aren't useful without erasing their existing records.

14 / ACCOUNT

Export, security and support

Use email or Google sign-in, manage business and billing settings, download your workspace in JSON, request account deletion and contact the founders through the support area.

A few important boundaries

Cerium is a business operations workspace, not a bank or accounting firm. The present bank control does not establish a live bank feed; invoice templates do not replace country-specific e-invoicing or tax filing; and the software does not send bulk SMS or review requests on your behalf. Features involving payments, phone codes and approved email reminders also depend on their configured providers. Terms and Privacy explain these responsibilities.

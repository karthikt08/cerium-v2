Privacy Policy.

What information Cerium collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain words.

At a glance

  • Cerium processes account, business, contact and workspace information so you can use the service.
  • Payment-method details are collected by Razorpay, not by Cerium. A live bank-feed integration is not currently available.
  • We do not sell personal information or use Cerium workspace records for advertising.
  • Signed-in users can export workspace information or request account deletion in Settings; legal and backup retention exceptions may apply.

Scope and privacy contact

This policy explains how Cerium (“we”, “us”) handles personal information through our website, account registration, subscription and contractor-operations workspace. It also describes what may happen when a business user uploads information about their own customers. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Cerium operator at cerium.founders@gmail.com or via our contact page. Where data-protection law distinguishes controllers/fiduciaries from processors, Cerium decides how its own account and security data is used, while business customers decide why their customer records are stored in their workspace.

Information we process

  • Identity and login: name, email, account identifiers, account settings, authentication history and session records. Email/password registration uses a password hash rather than storing the original password. For Google sign-in, we receive account identity information from Google but not your Google password.
  • Business setup and eligibility: business or trading name, service category, contact details, operating country, address, state/region, postal code and time zone, and any tax-registration details you enter. We process the verified phone number, phone-verification status and related attempts, and the country/pricing information associated with your account. A phone code does not verify the legal existence of a company.
  • Workspace content: leads, customer names and contact details, service and job addresses, notes, sales estimates, approval status, scheduled jobs, crew assignments, invoice line items, payment-status and transaction records, reviews, follow-up dates and optional messages to support. This can include other people's personal information if you enter it.
  • Spreadsheet imports: the CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file that you select is parsed to suggest a mapping and preview leads. Imported lead fields are saved only after you confirm. Avoid including unnecessary or sensitive columns in uploaded spreadsheets.
  • Billing: subscription price tier, currency, authorisation state, provider reference numbers and related records. Razorpay processes the payment method in its checkout; Cerium does not request or store a full card number, banking password or UPI secret.
  • Reminders and responses: when used, destination email addresses, reminder approvals, scheduled times, delivery status and opt-out choices; customer quote-approval decisions and tokens or references needed for secure approval links.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, request and session information and security/rate-limit indicators. Our IP-intelligence provider, ipapi.is, receives the relevant IP address to assess country and reported VPN/proxy/Tor indicators; the server temporarily caches access decisions. These predictions may be imperfect.

Why we use information

We use information to register and authenticate accounts; verify phones; establish region-based subscription eligibility; create and maintain business records; process approved reminders; support customer-facing approval links; generate invoice views; provide export and deletion controls; handle subscription payments; answer support requests; investigate misuse; and meet legal obligations. Depending on the jurisdiction and activity, the applicable legal basis may include performance of a contract, a legal duty, our legitimate interests in safe operation, your consent or another basis allowed by law. Where consent is required, you may withdraw it through available controls or by contacting us, subject to legal limitations.

We do not use customer lists, jobs or invoice details for targeted advertising or sell them to data brokers. We do not claim that our internal industry-based workspace suggestions use a remote AI model; current suggestions run locally from your selected category and description.

Ask Cerium assistant: the optional navigation assistant can answer from a Cerium navigation guide or a small trained intent classifier running on our server. If the operator enables external or self-hosted generative AI answers, the question you submit, the current workspace section and general Cerium product documentation are sent to Cerium's configured model to generate a reply. With self-hosted Ollama these prompts stay on Cerium's server; if OpenAI is selected, these inputs are transmitted to OpenAI as an external service. We do not automatically include your customer lists, invoices, bank records or workspace content in these requests. Do not submit private customer details, payment credentials or other sensitive information in the assistant. If an external AI provider is selected, that provider processes submitted questions under its applicable service and privacy terms. Assistant messages are not added to your business records by Cerium.

Customer information you put in the workspace

Business users control which customer information they enter, import or share via invoice/quote links. They are responsible for giving any notices and obtaining permissions required to collect, upload or send that information. Cerium processes it to provide their selected workflows and maintain account security. If you are the customer of a business using Cerium, contact that business first about the accuracy or use of a record; you can also write to us for help handling a request.

Recipients and service providers

We disclose the information needed for the requested task to hosting and infrastructure providers, Google (when you use Google sign-in and when external Google Fonts are loaded), Razorpay (subscription checkout and billing), a configured phone-verification provider such as Twilio (verification codes), configured email-delivery providers (if approved reminders are enabled), and ipapi.is (IP classification). We may also share information in response to lawful requests, to prevent abuse, or in a genuine business reorganisation subject to applicable safeguards. External websites such as Google review pages process information under their own terms once you choose to open them.

Bank connections: the current Cerium workspace does not perform a live bank-account connection or download transactions from a bank. The displayed connection control and sync action do not retrieve real bank-feed data. We do not ask for your online banking credentials through this flow. Recorded payment and transaction information may still appear in a workspace.

Cookies, sign-in and external resources

Cerium uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. During a Google OAuth sign-in flow, a short-lived state cookie may also be used. These are functional cookies, not behavioural advertising cookies. Google or Razorpay can set their own cookies when you use their pages. Some public pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may disclose connection metadata such as your IP address to Google. Basic server request logs and short-term anti-abuse counters may be processed to maintain the service.

Where information may be processed

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you or your customers live. We do not promise that all data stays in one country. Where cross-border transfer safeguards or restrictions apply, processing and provider arrangements must meet those requirements.

Retention and account deletion

We generally retain account and workspace data while your account remains active or until you delete it. When you use the account-deletion control, Cerium removes the account and associated workspace data from its live database after checking that active provider subscriptions can be cancelled. The provider may retain its own payment records. Backups are rotated separately, and legally necessary financial, security or dispute records may need longer retention. We do not promise a fixed post-trial deletion period: read-only workspace records may remain available for export after the trial ends.

Short-lived sign-in and verification records expire according to their technical purpose and configuration; some abuse-prevention records are retained for longer if needed to protect the service. Contact us about a specific retention question.

Your privacy choices and requests

From your signed-in Settings, you can update supported profile fields, download a JSON export of the current account/workspace details, cancel recurring billing, and use the Delete my data control to request full account deletion. A JSON export is not necessarily a copy of every log or provider-held payment record. You can also ask us to review, correct or delete information, request an accessible copy, object or withdraw consent where applicable, or exercise other rights available under your local law. We may verify your identity and may retain information that the law requires us to keep.

For requests, corrections, concerns or grievances, email cerium.founders@gmail.com. We will review requests in light of applicable law. If you linked a Google account, deleting your Cerium account does not revoke the connection in Google's settings; you can separately remove it there.

How we protect information

We use account-scoped database access, password hashing for email logins, signed session handling, access controls, server-side request checks, rate limiting and other reasonable safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use strong credentials, protect shared devices, and report suspected misuse to our support email.

Children

Cerium is intended for business users aged 18 or older, not for children. Do not submit information about minors unless it is lawful and necessary for a legitimate business purpose. Contact us if you believe a minor has registered.

Updates and contact

We may update this notice as the Service or legal obligations change. The date above identifies this version, dated October 26, 2026. For material changes, we will provide notice where required by law. Contact cerium.founders@gmail.com with any privacy question.

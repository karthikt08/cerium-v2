Privacy Policy.

What information Cerium collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain words.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

On this page

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run your workspace.
  • Your bank connection is optional and read-only. We never see your bank password.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can download or delete your data anytime. Today you can do both yourself, on the waitlist page.

What we collect

When you use Cerium, we collect these kinds of information:

  • Account details. You join and log in with your Google account. Google shares your name and email address with us. Google also offers a profile photo, but we do not save it. We never see your Google password, and we do not keep any Google access token. We do not ask for your EIN or Social Security number to sign up.
  • Waitlist details. If you join our waitlist, we save your name and email address from your Google account, the date you joined, which version of these terms you accepted, and (if you choose) your type of business. We use them only to tell you when Cerium opens. We keep them in our own database. You can download or delete them yourself on the waitlist page at any time.
  • Sign-in record. To keep you signed in, our server keeps a short-lived record. It holds your Google ID, your name, and your email address. It ends when you sign out, when it runs out after 15 days, or when you delete your data.
  • Trial details. The date your free trial started.
  • What you add to Cerium. Leads, job notes, follow-up dates, invoices, and payment records. This can include your customers’ names, phone numbers, email addresses, and job addresses.
  • Payment information, only if you subscribe. Our payment provider, Stripe, collects your card details on its own secure page. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Bank information, only if you connect your bank. Read-only details, such as deposits and other transactions, that our bank connection provider shares with us.
  • Basic technical information. To keep the site safe and stop abuse, our server uses your IP address to limit how many requests one computer can make, and Oracle Cloud, which hosts our server, may log it for a short time. We do not use advertising or analytics tools.

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • run Cerium for you: save your leads, show your reminders, create invoices and PDFs, and suggest payment matches;
  • manage your free trial, your plan, and your billing;
  • answer your questions and send important notices about your account;
  • keep Cerium safe, fix problems, and make it better;
  • follow the law when we have to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these cases:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us run Cerium, such as hosting, sign-in, payments, bank connections, email, and text messaging. Today this includes Google (sign-in and fonts) and our hosting provider. When the app opens, it will also include Stripe (payments). They can use your information only to do their job for us.
  • Service providers. Companies that help us run Cerium, such as hosting, sign-in, payments, bank connections, email, and text messaging. Today this includes Google (sign-in and fonts) and Oracle Cloud (hosting). When the app opens, it may also include a payment provider. They can use your information only to do their job for us.
  • A sale of Cerium. If Cerium is sold or merged with another company, your information may go to the new owner. We would tell you.

Your customers’ information

You choose what customer information to add to Cerium, and you stay in charge of it. We use it only to run Cerium for you.

You are responsible for having the right to add it, and for following the laws that apply when you contact your customers, such as the rules for texts and emails.

Bank connections

Connecting your bank is optional. When you connect, a third-party provider handles your bank login. Cerium gets read-only access to the details it needs to suggest which deposit pays which invoice, and you confirm each match.

We never see or store your bank password. You can turn the connection off whenever you want.

How long we keep it

While you are on the waitlist, we keep your details until we send you the launch email, or until you delete them. When you press Delete my data, we delete them from our live database right away and sign you out on every device. Copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days. We may keep some records longer if the law requires it, such as billing records.

Your choices

On the waitlist page you can download a copy of your details, or delete them for good. Log in with Google to get there. For anything else, such as fixing your details or asking what we hold about you, email us and we will help.

Deleting your data does not remove Cerium from the list of apps connected to your Google Account. You can remove it yourself in your Google Account, under Security, in “Your connections to third-party apps & services”.

If you live in a state with privacy laws, such as California, you may have more rights, like asking what information we collected about you. We may need to confirm it is you before we act on a request.

Security and cookies

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No online service is 100% safe, so we cannot promise perfect security. Please keep your Google account secure.

We use one cookie, only to keep you signed in. The site needs it to work. We do not use it for advertising or tracking, and we do not use analytics or advertising cookies. While you sign in, Google may set its own cookies on its own pages. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page opens. You can block cookies in your browser, but then you cannot sign in.

Children

Cerium is made for businesses. It is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top of this page. If a change is important, we will tell you by email or inside Cerium.

Contact us

Questions about privacy, or want to use your choices above? Email us at cerium.founders@gmail.com or use our Contact page.
